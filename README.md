# The World Today — Tuesday, 24 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Tuesday, 24 November 2026, rendered with Pillow into a single 1024×1024 image. The lead story is Nvidia's board reinstating Jensen Huang as chief executive after just 11 days — a reversal under pressure from President Trump and Vice President Vance, cleared by a US government review that found "no national security risk", sending Nvidia shares up 31–33% and the Nasdaq up 6%. Around it: the US blacklisting Singapore chip firm Megaspeed, SK hynix up 20%, Iran striking Gulf sites as sanctions tighten, Russian jets entering South Korea's air defence zone, a deadly drone attack on the Kyiv region, Ukraine's claimed sinking of a Russian warship off Libya, and Mexico's capture of "El Chapo Isidro".

`gen.py` draws the front page (deterministic, no randomness or timestamps); `main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (stdlib only).
